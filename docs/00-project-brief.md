# 00 — Project Brief: Last Orbit (v0.2, 2026-10-25)

Working title: **Last Orbit: Solar System Showdown** (provisional). Owner & creative decision-maker: **Gábor**.

## One-line pitch

Eight planetary civilizations race the final orbit of a dying, furious Sun: a 2–5 player social risk card game where you sabotage rivals, contain solar catastrophes, and try to be the last flagship still flying — playable in 10–20 minutes, teachable in five.

## Origin & relationship to references

- **Emotional inspiration:** the shrinking-deck tension and table talk of *Exploding Kittens* (mechanics referenced, IP never copied — see `reference/exploding-kittens-notes.md` and the separation rules below).
- **Fictional home:** Gábor's Solar System universe (the Explorer project). The game is a sibling, not a child: same cosmic neighborhood, completely independent operation.
- **Product ambition (Gábor, session 1):** a web app/site with cool stylized graphics and animations, eventually **online multiplayer**. Digital-first, not a board game port.

## Creative pillars (ranked)

1. **The Sun is the villain.** All captains share one doom. Elimination is a flagship being disabled and towed home — comedy of consequence, never extermination.
2. **Escalate to a finale.** Every match must end *hot*. The Sun Fury module guarantees rising stakes as the deck shrinks.
3. **One rule per card.** Every card is a single sentence a stranger can execute. Complexity budget is spent on interaction, not on card text.
4. **Readable drama.** Whose turn, what was played, who's targeted, how many turns are owed, how many cards remain — always legible at a glance. The app enforces the rules so players never argue them.
5. **Laugh together, not at each other.** Humour comes from characters, situations, animations, and consequences — never from mocking the player.
6. **Grounded cosmic comedy.** Loosely real planetary characteristics (Venus's acid clouds, Uranus's tilt) turned into comedy premises. Clever, not childish.

## Audience

- Primary: adult groups & game nights (3–5 players), casual and non-gamers included.
- Secondary: families (age 10+), couples (2-player mode must work).
- Platform: mobile & desktop web.

## Tone

Funny, character-driven, cinematic-cool. Think "ocean liner band playing as the ship lists" energy: competence under absurd pressure.

## Success criteria (validation gates)

- Rules teachable in ≤5 minutes by a non-designer.
- Median match 10–20 min (2p may be shorter); ≤2 min elimination downtime (or Derelict module solves it).
- Playtest medians (1–5 scales): Fun ≥4, Tension ≥4, Clarity ≥4, Agency ≥3.5, Humour ≥3.5, "would replay" ≥4/5 for ≥60% of testers.
- No dominant single strategy; win-rate spread across seats <15% in simulation.
- Digital: state always readable; rules never argued because the app is the judge.

## What this is NOT

- Not a reskin of Exploding Kittens: original premise, vocabulary, card identities, mechanics additions (Sun Fury, Derelicts, factions), art, and audio.
- Not scientifically accurate: real astronomy is a comedy well, not a constraint.
- Not monetized yet: that decision is postponed past Phase 4 (see roadmap).

## Separation charter (Solar System Explorer)

The Explorer is never modified, redesigned, deployed, or documented by this project. No shared repo, roadmap, or code. Reuse of *specific techniques* (planet shading, orbital math, camera feel) may be **evaluated** in Phase 5 by reading only, recorded as a decision. Default stance (D-009): **"new world, borrowed sparks"** — this game gets its own stylized, cinematic visual identity.
