# 05 — Signature Mechanic Analysis (v0.2, 2026-10-25)

Question: what is *Last Orbit's* signature mechanic? Candidates: **Sun Fury** (new, proposed) vs **Reactor Charge** (master-prompt proposal). Decision D-003: Sun Fury is the lead; Reactor Charge remains a faction-layer candidate.

## Sun Fury (proposed signature)

**Rule:** each containment enrages the Sun (+1 Fury, max 5). Higher Fury shrinks the legal reinsertion depth for the contained Rupture: ≤1 anywhere → 15 → 10 → 7 → 5.

**Why it works:**
- *Theme-mechanics unity:* the Sun is the villain; its temper literally compresses your options.
- *Escalation guarantee:* the endgame cannot fizzle; the finale always happens at high tension. Fixes the reference's flat-endgame weakness.
- *Readability:* one number, one rule, one look. It's teachable in one sentence.
- *Digital spectacle for free:* the Sun visibly flares each +1; reinsertion UI shows the shrinking "safe zone" — the mechanic *is* the animation.
- *Balanced by design:* Fury only rises through survivals (which already feel good), so it taxes the winners of small victories, naturally rubber-banding the match.

**Risks:** depth 5 can feel brutal (mitigate: cap reached late); tracking depth is invisible work in physical play (fine — digital-first).

## Reactor Charge (master prompt proposal)

**Rule (as proposed):** sacrifice a useful card to generate Charge; Charge powers your civilization's special ability; opponents can estimate but don't know what was sacrificed.

**Assessment:** strong *faction* mechanic, weak *signature* mechanic: it multiplies per-civilization content (each power needs design, balance, UI), it competes with cards rather than structuring the match arc, and its fun depends entirely on the faction layer existing. It also adds a second resource before the first module is validated.

## Comparison (1–5, higher = better)

| Criterion | Sun Fury | Reactor Charge |
|---|---|---|
| Complexity economy | **5** (one number) | 2 (resource × 8 faction powers) |
| Originality vs reference | **4** (no counterpart) | 4 (no counterpart) |
| Thematic value | **5** (Sun = villain = difficulty curve) | 3 (engine room flavor) |
| Balance predictability | **4** (monotone escalation, sim-friendly) | 2 (8 powers to tune) |
| Readability | **5** | 2 (hidden estimates) |
| Digital presentation | **5** (the Sun is the UI) | 3 |

**Verdict:** Sun Fury as the match-level signature; Reactor Charge reconsidered in P2 as the *faction* mechanic (where hidden sacrifices and civilization identity live). Final call after PT-03. *(P2 prep, 2026-10-25: the re-evaluation menu — drop / currency / hybrid — is drafted in `14` §Reactor Charge; final call unchanged.)*

Pass criteria: median match length stays 10–20 min; "clarity" ≥4 despite the module; sim shows Fury 5 reached in <25% of matches (else escalation too fast); players describe the ending as "tense" more often than "unfair".
