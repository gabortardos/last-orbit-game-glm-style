# 06 — Visual & Audio Direction Brief (v0.2, 2026-10-25)

This is a **brief**, not art. Production begins in Phase 3, after rules validate. Gábor's mandate: "cool graphics and animations" — this brief ensures we know what "cool" means before anyone opens a 3D editor.

## Art direction (headline)

**"Cinema poster solar system":** a stylized, glowing, silk-screen-inspired cosmos — flat-but-luminous planets, dramatic rim light, deep blacks, one loud accent color per civilization. NOT the educational-realism of the Explorer (different product, different look — D-009).

- **Reference energy (moods, not copies):** retro space-program posters; mid-century sci-fi book covers; neon-noir contrast; the dramatic silhouette language of racing livery.
- **The Sun is a character:** the visual protagonist. Calm = soft gold corona. Fury 2 = licking flares. Fury 5 = screaming white-orange maw filling the sky. The Sun Fury meter is *the same object*, not a HUD widget.
- **Captains & flagships:** silhouette-first design (recognizable in one glance at 48px). Ship silhouettes echo their home planet's identity (Venus = floating mask-barge in golden acid clouds; Saturn = ring-winged corsair frigate…). Expressive idle animations sell personality.
- **Cards:** icon-first, colour-plus-shape coded (colour-blind safe), big text-safe zones, one sentence per card. Card backs: black with a gold solar corona motif.
- **Layout:** 2D glass-panel UI over 3D vignettes. Spectacle never hides state (turn owner, pending action, targets, debt, deck count — always visible; Pillar 4).

## Motion language

1. **Orbital drift** — everything not actively happening drifts slowly along orbit lines (life, calm).
2. **Flare punctuation** — dramatic actions cut the calm: a rupture draw is a solar flare that washes the screen for 300ms (reduced-motion: a single static flash frame + haptic/audio cue instead).
3. **Camera grammar** — slow push-ins on tension (deck shrinking), snap-zoom on Overrides, wide contemplative drift on Scan peeks.
4. **Physical comedy** — disabled flagships tumble, eject coffee mugs, get towed by a tiny stern tugboat drone. Defeat is funny, never gruesome (Pillar 5).
5. **Derelicts** — ghost-blue hologram ships circling the Sun, one antenna blinking while their Final Transmission is armed.

## Audio direction

- **Bed:** low sub drone whose intensity tracks Sun Fury (the soundtrack *is* the doom clock).
- **Stingers:** rupture reveal (solar roar + sudden vacuum silence), containment slam (metallic thunk + power-down whine), Override (radio squelch), Flyby (afterburner whoosh).
- **Character voices:** garbled radio babble per civilization (tone-based, no real language) — comedy through delivery.
- **Accessibility:** all audio cues mirrored visually; all motion cues available as reduced-motion variants; colour never the only signal.

## Deliverables when Phase 3 opens

1. Style frames (6 key moments: setup, scan, rupture draw, containment reinsert, flyby chain, victory tow).
2. Sun Fury state chart (0→5 visual/audio behaviour table).
3. One civilization design sheet (Mercury pilot) proving the pipeline.
4. Motion prototype: deck-count → camera tension link.
